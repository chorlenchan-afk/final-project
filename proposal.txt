RQ: Are international migrants and refugees both primarily hosted by the countries of the Global North? 
My major is sociology, so I chose a sociological topic for this final project. People often believe primarily refugees and immigrants go to countries of the Global North, but migration theory argues that international migration requires resources, so international migrants are not the poorest and they are more likely to the global north while refugees are more likely to neighboring countries. Based on this, I expect immigrants mostly towards Global North, but refugees are mainly stay in the Global South, and I will to use data to test this.
I define the global north using a classification file from UNCTAD. And I also use two files: refugees by country of asylum and total number of international immigrants to find the refugees and immigrants destination. The data is from UNHCR and UN DESA respectively. I will visualize the refugees and immigrants hosted by the countries of the Global North and by the countries of the Global South respectively.
The data of immigrants covers 1990-2024, and the data of refugees covers 1951-2025, I will use the overlapping period (1990-2024) to compare the data.
